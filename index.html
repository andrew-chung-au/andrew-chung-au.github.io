Background

Before the transition

I spent over a decade in higher education in Hong Kong. At the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology I led the technical delivery and systems integration of an assessment platform used by more than 3,000 people across two universities, and applied Rasch statistical modelling to large-scale assessment data. I then taught at City University of Hong Kong, where I was Course Lead responsible for standardisation across a large course.

Seeing how quickly AI was developing, I decided to move into the field. My technical foundation is in cyber security, through a Certificate IV that included a penetration test and a red, blue and purple team exercise. That led into cloud, which I took further by earning the AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate. Alongside that, and since, I've been building the AI and data projects on this page.
